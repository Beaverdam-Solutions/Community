# B-002 aide-memoire 2/7 — Criteria for sifting the parent framework

## Operator's concern

The central question on his mind: how to sift through all of ESACP (the
parent project's ~118-session governance and tooling corpus), discarding
everything redundant, misguided, obsolete, or broken while preserving —
restructured, reclassified, corrected, improved — the parts actually needed.
The criteria must be understood in terms of a dual Mission and dual Vision:
one for Beaverdam as a community, one for the software in the hands of an
(at the time) ill-defined Buzz.

## Roger's response

Invert the sift. Artifact-by-artifact review of the parent corpus is
supply-driven and biased toward keeping (everything there earned its place
against that project's own failures). Instead: write the dual Missions and
Visions first, derive Beaverdam's actual requirements from them, and pull
from the parent only what answers a derived requirement. **Null hypothesis:
discard.** The two M&Vs act as two distinct sieves — the community sieve
pulls trust-and-accountability machinery; the software-in-Buzz's-hands sieve
pulls engineering practice (reclassified as such, not as governance).
Anything neither sieve pulls stays behind, still valid for the parent.

**Status: approved in action** — the operator's next move was "the central
issue we need to resolve before anything else is nailing down Mission and
Vision," and the rest of the session executed exactly that.

## Residue

The actual sift (pulling specific parent artifacts through the sieves) has
not begun; the sieves now exist (README + internal_docs/mission_and_vision.md).
